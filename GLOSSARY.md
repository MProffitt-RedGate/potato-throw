# Glossary

**Game**
The single shared world of one potato and its players. Played asynchronously: players launch the application now and then, rather than at the same time.

**Player**
A person who has been added to the Game and can hold the Potato.

**Name**
The display name a Player chooses for themselves on first launch. It is how other Players refer to them when inviting or throwing.

**Potato**
The one object in the Game. At any moment exactly one Player holds it.

**Holder**
The Player who currently has the Potato. Only the Holder can Throw it.

**Throw**
The Holder passing the Potato to another chosen Player. The recipient becomes the Holder, and finds out the next time they launch the application.

**Invite**
Adding a person to the Game's list of Players. It sends no message; the inviter tells the invitee themselves.

**First Player**
The Player who starts the Game. They are the first Holder.

**Claim**
A new Player taking one of the invited-but-unclaimed Names on their first launch. The Name is then theirs for good.

**Throw history**
The record of past Throws. Launch screens show the last 5.
